Honest limitations

  • Synthetic, clean renders. Images are procedurally drawn; real photos add perspective, blur and glare. The factory measures a lower bound on real-world difficulty.
  • The verifier knows the rendering conventions (palette, dial geometry, terminal positions). It is independent of the evaluated model and of the generator's answer, but not of the drawing style. Changing a renderer means updating its verifier; the test-suite enforces agreement.
  • Prompt sensitivity. One fixed prompt and answer format per task; results can move a few points with other prompts. Unparsed answers are reported separately and count as wrong.
  • Sample sizes. Per-cell pair counts are small in the demo run; treat single cells as indicative, use the generator to scale up (cost is linear and cached).
  • Difficulty levels are ordinal, designed per family; L3 in one family is not calibrated to equal L3 in another.

Reproduce: pixelproof run --seed 7 --pairs 4. Audit: pixelproof audit demo re-derives every answer from pixels and checks image hashes.
